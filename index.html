The sequence balances grant value, building fabric and the longer-term aim of relying less on grid electricity.

2

Make Manton fabric-ready

Commission a fabric-first assessment, then use the 0% VAT window for qualifying insulation, controls and draught-proofing before final heat-pump sizing.

Price solar PV plus battery storage at both homes

Treat generation and storage as the practical path toward greater electricity-grid independence. Installer-supplied solar PV and standalone batteries qualify for 0% VAT until 31 March 2027.

The damp question

No dedicated owner-occupier grant was found for damp-proofing, PIV or mould remediation.

For Thurso: treat humidity-controlled extraction, PIV or MVHR as self-funded work. Keep trickle vents and air bricks clear, and consider qualifying insulation work under the 0% VAT window.

Important boundary: standalone ventilation units are not listed for the VAT relief. Draught stripping is covered when supplied and fitted as qualifying work.

Caveats and source trail

The remaining checks, exclusions and evidence behind the assessment.

Remaining questions and resolved checks

Manton gas-grid status: regular oil deliveries strongly suggest the house is off-grid, but this must be confirmed before relying on the £9,000 BUS rate.

Warm Homes: Local Grant: DN21 4JT is not in the official February 2026 eligible-postcodes list. With household income above £36,000 and no qualifying benefits, Manton does not meet any of the scheme's eligibility routes.

ECO4 status: Ofgem says 31 December 2026; other reporting conflicts. The household is not eligible under either reading.

North Lincolnshire oil support: a past scheme existed, but no current open round was confirmed.

What the 0% VAT relief does not cover

New, double or secondary glazing remains at 20%. DIY materials bought directly by the homeowner remain at 20%. Conventional boilers, associated radiators and pipework remain at 20%. Insulation installed as part of a new extension does not receive the relief.
